## How to Test Your Translation

1. Save your translated words into a UTF-8 `.json` file (e.g. `es.json` or `de.json`).
2. Open OntyTask and click **Menu -> Language -> Import translation JSON...**.
3. Choose your file to test it immediately.
4. Share it on our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Agzes/OntyTask/issues/new?template=translations.yml)!
