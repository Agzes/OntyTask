# Getting Started with OntyTask

Here's how to quickly get up and running with OntyTask!

---

## 1. How It Works

OntyTask is completely portable:

- **No Installation:** Download and run it from any folder. No setup wizards or administrator rights needed.
- **Single Window:** If OntyTask is already open and you launch it again, it brings your existing window to the front.
- **Saves Your Settings:** Hotkeys, speed, loops, and theme are saved automatically to `OntyTask.ini` right next to the executable.
- **Safe Window Position:** If you disconnect a secondary monitor, the window automatically recenters onto your active display.

---

## 2. Interface and Buttons

The OntyTask toolbar provides quick access to all essential actions:

- **Open:** Select and load a saved `.onty` macro file.
- **Save:** Save your current recording to a `.onty` file.
- **Record:** Click to start recording (turns into a white square while recording; click again to stop).
- **Play:** Click to play your macro (turns into a white square while playing; click again to cancel).
- **Settings:** Open settings for speed multipliers, playback loops, themes, custom hotkeys, and language.

---

## 3. How to Record & Play

### Recording a Macro

1. Press the Record hotkey (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R</kbd> by default) or click **Record**.
2. OntyTask starts capturing your keyboard and mouse actions.
3. Perform your workflow or game actions.
4. Press the Record hotkey again (or click Stop). The final stop shortcut is automatically omitted from the macro.

### Playing It Back

1. Press the Play hotkey (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Alt+P</kbd> by default) or click **Play**.
2. The cursor moves to your starting position, and actions replay smoothly.
3. OntyTask repeats the macro for your chosen number of loops.
4. **Need to stop?** Press your panic stop key (<kbd>Pause</kbd>, <kbd>Scroll Lock</kbd>) or move your physical mouse!
